Hybrid Intelligent Solar Dryer

Version 1 combines solar PV support, battery storage, controlled auxiliary heat and forced-convection airflow in an enclosed chamber.

30 kgbatch capacity
40–85°Ctemperature range
~15 hstated PV autonomy
53 kgprototype weight

Dimensions: ~1.5 × 1.0 × 1.8 m. V2 focuses on airflow, insulation, controls, efficiency, user interface, pilots and local small-scale production.
